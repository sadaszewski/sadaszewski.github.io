Every base keeps a fixed address on the sheet — row and column never move. Zoom and pan scale the sheet in both axes; they never rewrap it. Changing bp / row is the only thing that does.

The colour dropdown substitutes what the sheet encodes. Where an annotation has many members — transcripts, species — a second dropdown picks the one on screen. Nothing stacks.
